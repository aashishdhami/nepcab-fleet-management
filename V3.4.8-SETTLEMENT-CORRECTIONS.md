# NepCab v3.4.8 — Settlement corrections

## Changes
- Added Edit and Delete actions to Driver Payroll settlement history.
- Added Edit and Delete actions to Owner Settlements history.
- Editing updates the existing payment record's amount, BS payment date, and payroll/settlement period. Driver payment notes can also be corrected.
- Deleting asks for confirmation and removes the selected payment record.
- Existing payroll and owner settlement balances are recalculated from the stored payment records on the next render/load; earnings and commission calculations are not changed.

## Important
- These are payment-record corrections, not bank-transfer actions. Confirm the real-world payment before editing or deleting a record.
- The current implementation uses the existing payment tables and delete behaviour; it does not add a separate audit-log history for prior values.
- Test in a safe/test dataset first, including changing the amount and period and deleting a test payment.
