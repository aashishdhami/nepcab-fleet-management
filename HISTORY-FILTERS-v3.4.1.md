# NepCab v3.4.1 — Booking & Vehicle Reading History

## Multi-day booking history
- Added BS month filtering and All months option.
- Added search across booking details, vehicle, driver and owner.
- Added custom BS export date range and CSV export with booking amount and saved commission fields.

## Daily vehicle reading history
- Replaced the vehicle dropdown filter with a single registration/driver search field.
- Added BS month filtering and All months option.
- Added custom BS export date range and CSV export including odometer, battery and next-reading interval information.

No database schema changes are required. Existing records are unchanged.
